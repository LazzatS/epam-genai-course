    How the counts were made

    • Scope: the introduction, sections 1 to 10 and the conclusion of the report. The title, contents list and link addresses are not counted.
    • Explicit: an automatic search for the term, including its plural, abbreviation or short form. "Human agents" and "agent-assist" are excluded, because they mean people.
    • Implicit: phrases chosen by hand that express the idea without the term. Every phrase was checked automatically against the report, and each one is quoted in the evidence list.
    • Limit: implicit counts depend on my judgment, so they show how I read the report rather than an exact measure. One phrase can support two terms.
